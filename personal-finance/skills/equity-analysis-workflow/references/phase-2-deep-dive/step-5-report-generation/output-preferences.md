# Output Preferences

When you write the report:

### Font

- Use "San Francisco (Pro)" (SF Pro) font, or system font.

### Writing style

- Use bullet/ordered points wherever possible.
- Split into smaller paragraphs. For example, 2-3 shorter focused paragraphs in a section rather than 4-6 sentence blocks.

### Presentation

- Visualize at least the historical or comparable figures, like growth rate, margin trend etc (Unless instructed to skip).
